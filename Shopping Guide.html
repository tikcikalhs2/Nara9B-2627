  Shopping Guide!

 

Welcome to NERSHKA! We bring you an exclusive curation of top-tier footwear from world-renowned brands, including Adidas, Onitsuka Tiger, Salomon, and Nike.

Follow this step-by-step guide to ensure a smooth, secure, and hassle-free shopping experience on our website.

Step 1: Browse and Discover (Index Menu)

  • Head over to the Index page to explore our latest arrivals, bestsellers, and featured collections.

  • Use our category filters or search bar to find your favorite brands: Adidas, Onitsuka Tiger, Salomon, or Nike.

  • Click on any product image to view detailed information, including available sizes, colorways, high-resolution photos, and pricing.

Step 2: Select Your Size & Add to Cart

  • Refer to our size chart (available on each product page) to find your perfect fit. We provide standard US, UK, and EU sizing conversions.

  • Select your preferred size and quantity.

  • Click "Add to Cart" to save the item to your shopping bag, or click "Buy Now" to proceed directly to checkout.

Step 3: Review Your Order

  • Click on the Cart icon at the top right of the page to review your selected items.

  • Here, you can update quantities, remove items, or apply any promotional discount codes you might have.

  • Once everything looks correct, click "Proceed to Checkout."

Step 4: Shipping & Customer Information

  • Enter your complete shipping details, including:

    • Full Name

    • Active Phone Number (for courier contact)

    • Complete Delivery Address (Street, City, Postal Code, Country)

  • Double-check your address to ensure timely and accurate delivery.

Step 5: Secure Payment

  • Choose your preferred payment method from our secure checkout options (e.g., Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer, E-Wallets, or Cash on Delivery where applicable).

  • Complete the payment authorization. Once successful, you will receive an Order Confirmation Email containing your unique Order ID.

Step 6: Order Tracking & Delivery

  • Our team will carefully inspect and package your sneakers within 1–2 business days.

  • Once shipped, you will receive a follow-up email with a Tracking Number so you can monitor your package's journey right to your doorstep.
